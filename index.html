Grammar Focus

Past Continuous

We use the Past Continuous to talk about actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past. It sets the scene.

[ WAS / WERE ] + [ VERB-ING ]

  • 🍟 "Joey was eating his food."
  • 🍷 "They were drinking wine at the restaurant."

Grammar Focus

The Interruption

What happens when a long action is stopped by a sudden action? We use WHEN + Past Simple.

Long Action: "I was eating my burger..." (Past Continuous)

WHEN

Sudden Action: "...she took my fries." (Past Simple)

Syntax

The Formula

1. The Background

Sets the scene (Past Continuous).

"We were talking and laughing..."

2. The Problem

The sudden event (Past Simple).

"...when she suddenly reached for my plate."
